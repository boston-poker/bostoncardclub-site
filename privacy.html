Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-23

This privacy policy applies to a personal automation tool operated by the owner of this website. The tool is used to upload generated audio files to Google Drive and to support a private podcast publishing workflow.

Data we access

With your Google account permission, the app may access Google Drive data required to:

  • Upload MP3 audio files generated by the automation workflow
  • Store and retrieve metadata related to those uploads (file IDs, names, sizes)
  • Organize uploaded files within a specific Drive folder owned by the operator

How we use the data

Drive access is used only to perform the upload and organization steps for the operator’s publishing workflow. The app does not sell data and does not use Drive data for advertising.

Data sharing

The app does not share Google Drive data with third parties, except as required to operate Google APIs.

Data retention

Uploaded audio files and any related metadata are retained in the operator’s Google Drive account and on the operator’s server. The operator may delete files at any time.

Security

The app uses OAuth to access Google APIs and stores credentials/tokens needed for automated operation. Access is limited to the operator’s account.
